Privacy Policy

We value and protect your personal privacy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Introduction

PawByteLabs (hereinafter referred to as "we") understands the importance of personal information to you. We will adopt corresponding security protection measures in accordance with legal requirements to protect your personal information to the best of our ability. This privacy policy will help you understand the following:

Google Play, account deletion, and third-party services

Google Play and our Android apps

This Privacy Policy applies to PawByteLabs mobile applications distributed on Google Play, including our wallpaper app (Android application ID: com.pawbytelabs.wallpaper). It describes how we access, collect, use, handle, and share user data in line with Google Play's User Data policy. The categories of data and practices we declare in the Google Play Console Data safety section are consistent with this policy; if you need app-version-specific details, refer to the Data safety section for the version you have installed.

Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your account or personal data associated with our apps by emailing feedback@pawbytelabs.com. We will verify your request and process deletion within a reasonable period, except where we must retain certain information to comply with applicable law or resolve disputes.

Third-party SDKs and service providers

Our apps may use third-party libraries or SDKs (for example, Google Play services, analytics, advertising, or crash reporting) only as needed to provide the service. How each category of data is used and shared is summarized in our Play Console Data safety declaration; third parties process data under their own privacy policies where they act as independent controllers or subprocessors.

Children's privacy

Our apps are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your region). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected such information, contact us and we will delete it promptly.

1. How We Collect and Use Your Personal Information

We collect and use your personal information for the following purposes described in this policy:

  • To provide you with our app services
  • To maintain and improve our services
  • To ensure service security
  • To comply with laws and regulations

Types of information we collect include:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers
  • Log information: app usage, crash logs, performance data
  • Location information: only collected with your explicit consent (required for certain features)
  • User-generated content: photos, videos, audio, etc. created while using the app

2. How We Store and Protect Your Personal Information

We adopt industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Use encryption technology (such as SSL/TLS) to protect data transmission
  • Encrypt stored sensitive data
  • Strictly limit access permissions; only authorized personnel can access personal information
  • Conduct regular security audits and vulnerability scans
  • Establish data backup and disaster recovery mechanisms

Your personal information will be stored on servers within US. We will only retain your personal information for the period necessary to achieve the purposes described in this policy.

3. How We Share, Transfer, and Publicly Disclose Your Personal Information

Sharing: We will not share your personal information with third parties unless:

  • We have obtained your explicit consent
  • To fulfill legal obligations or comply with government requirements
  • Sharing with our affiliates, but only to achieve the purposes described in this policy
  • Sharing with authorized partners, but we will require them to comply with this policy

Transfer: Without your explicit consent, we will not transfer your personal information to any company, organization or individual.

Public disclosure: We will only publicly disclose your personal information in the following circumstances:

  • With your explicit consent
  • Based on laws and regulations, legal processes, litigation, or mandatory requirements from government authorities

4. Your Rights

In accordance with relevant Chinese laws and regulations, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access: You have the right to access your personal information
  • Right to correction: You have the right to request us to correct or supplement your information
  • Right to deletion: In certain circumstances, you have the right to request us to delete your personal information
  • Withdraw consent: You have the right to withdraw your previous consent to us
  • Cancel account: You have the right to cancel your account

To exercise the above rights, please contact us through the contact information provided at the end of this policy.

5. How This Policy Is Updated

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When significant changes occur to the policy, we will notify you through the following methods:

  • Send notifications within the app
  • Post announcements on the website
  • Notify you by email

Without your explicit consent, we will not reduce your rights under this privacy policy.
